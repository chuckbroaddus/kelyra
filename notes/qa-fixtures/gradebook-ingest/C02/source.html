RIVERBEND ISD — SECONDARY GRADING GUIDELINES

Board Policy EIA (Local) · Adopted 2025

Grading periods and semester grades

The school year has four nine-weeks grading periods. Semester grade = 40% first nine weeks + 40% second nine weeks + 20% semester exam.

Grading scale

LetterRange
A90–100
B80–89
C70–79
FBelow 70

A semester average of 70 or higher is required to earn credit.

Parent communication

Progress reports are sent at the midpoint of each nine weeks.
