## Passage

Marisol kept a small notebook in her backpack. Each afternoon she wrote three things she noticed on the walk home: a cracked sidewalk tile, a dog that always barked twice, the smell of bread from the corner bakery. One Tuesday the bakery was closed. Marisol wrote that down too, then added, "Even missing things are worth noticing."

## Questions

1. What does Marisol keep in her backpack?
   - **Key:** a small notebook

2. How many things does she usually write each afternoon?
   - **Key:** three

3. What was different on Tuesday?
   - **Key:** the bakery was closed

4. In the last sentence, "missing things" most nearly means ____.
   - **Key:** things that are absent / not there

5. Identify the noun in: "The bakery smelled wonderful." (bakery / smelled / wonderful)
   - **Key:** bakery

6. Rewrite correctly: she walk home slow.
   - **Key:** She walks home slowly.

7. Is the passage fiction or a news report?
   - **Key:** fiction

8. One sentence: what theme does Marisol's note suggest?
   - **Key:** Paying attention matters, even when something is missing.
