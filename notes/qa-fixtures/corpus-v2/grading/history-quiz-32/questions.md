## Passage

In a fictional town called Maple Bend, residents wrote a short charter in 1785. The charter said leaders must listen to townsfolk before raising taxes, and that every free adult could speak at the monthly meeting. Historians use Maple Bend as a classroom model of early self-government — not a real founding document.
