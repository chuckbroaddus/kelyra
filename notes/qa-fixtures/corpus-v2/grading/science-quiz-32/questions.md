## Questions

1. Which organelle is often called the powerhouse of the cell?
   - **Key:** mitochondria

2. Plant cells make food using sunlight in a process called ____.
   - **Key:** photosynthesis

3. True/False: Animal cells have a cell wall.
   - **Key:** False

4. Name the jelly-like material that fills most of a cell.
   - **Key:** cytoplasm

5. On the plant-cell diagram, label A–D (nucleus, chloroplast, vacuole, cell wall).
   - **Key:** A nucleus; B chloroplast; C vacuole; D cell wall

6. What gas do plants release during photosynthesis?
   - **Key:** oxygen

7. DNA is stored mainly in which organelle?
   - **Key:** nucleus

8. One sentence: why do cells need energy?
   - **Key:** Cells need energy to grow, repair, and carry out life processes.
