# Community Service Project

**Objective:** Research and propose a small improvement for your local community. Present as a poster or slide deck (3-5 slides).

**Requirements:**
- Identify a local issue (traffic, park cleanliness, library hours, etc.)
- Gather at least 2 sources of information (interview, website, observation)
- Propose one realistic solution with steps
- Include a simple budget or timeline

**Due:** 2 weeks

---

## Rubric (20 points total)

- Issue identification & research (5 pts)
- Feasible proposal (5 pts)
- Presentation clarity (5 pts)
- Sources & creativity (5 pts)

**Teacher notes:** Students may work in pairs. Focus on civic engagement.
