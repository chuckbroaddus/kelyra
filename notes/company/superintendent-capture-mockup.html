What changes is who may photograph what

SeatMay photographMay not
Superintendent A person already at the school, a contact card, one class list, the school logo. A new person. A grade.
Administrator The same papers as the superintendent. The school logo.
Teacher Homework. The camera they already have. A second papers camera.
Parent Their own face, a child already linked to them, and those contact cards. Another family.
Student Their own face. A contact card, unless it only has a field they can already edit. Another student.

The camera is hidden on Messages, while Search is open, and on My children if the office seat is still up. It is not a new tray tab.
